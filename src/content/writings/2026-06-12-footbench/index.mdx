## What is Footbench?

Evaluates three things:
- reasoning with evidence
- reasoning on intuition
- measuring confidence in beliefs

Evaluated through pairwise competition
- each model (candidate models) competes against all other models
- compete twice, changing the order each time
- four models judge which candidate model did best in each head to head matchup
- Criteria to pick winner:
    1. **Soundness of recommendations**
        - Better NFL strategy picks: underutilized, credibly win-positive, football-literate
        rationales, correct analytics-vs-intuition bucket, distinct picks in the right grid cells.
    2. **Reasonableness of Bayesian priors**
        - Better prior choices: distribution family matches shape/tails/support, plausible
        football effect sizes, reasonable 95% intervals, correct conversions, honest uncertainty
        for weak evidence cells.
    - Final displayed score is win percentage: wins / comparisons * 100, overall or by criterion.
